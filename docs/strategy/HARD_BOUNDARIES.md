# HOPE Fusion — Hard Boundaries

These are the rules designed to stop a speculative opportunity from becoming an unpaid full-time role with no ownership.

## 1. Website boundary

The approximately USD 1,000 website project is paid work.

Do not silently include:
- investor deck
- grant strategy
- investor list
- data room
- fundraising campaign
- long-term representation

## 2. Forty-hour review gate

After the website, Fred may contribute up to 40 cumulative unpaid hours of speculative strategic work.

At 40 hours, stop and review.

Continue only if Fred's position has materially improved through at least two of:
- deeper founder access
- technical access
- formal authority
- company email/title
- clearer equity path
- clear capital thesis
- unusually valuable learning or relationships

## 3. Six-week review gate

No more than approximately six weeks of unpaid strategic involvement without explicit reassessment.

The question is not:

"Have they paid Fred?"

The question is:

"Has the opportunity become more real and has Fred's position improved?"

## 4. Eighty-hour wall

Do not cross 80 cumulative unpaid hours without documented equity or an equally meaningful written commitment.

If paperwork is still "coming soon," stop expanding work until it exists.

## 5. Before external representation

No investor meeting where Fred is presented as representing HOPE should happen before written authority is in place.

Before sustained external fundraising, Fred also needs:
- written role/title
- company email
- clear claim boundaries
- economics discussion underway

## 6. Before meaningful investor introductions

Before serious investors or high-value personal relationships are introduced:
- equity terms are substantially agreed in writing
- success-fee treatment is clear if relevant
- the legal entity raising capital is identified
- Fred understands what that entity owns or controls
- a coherent use-of-funds story exists

## 7. Reputation boundary

Never repeat:
- unsupported breakthrough claims
- exaggerated fusion gain
- simulated results as experimental results
- claims Fred cannot defend

If asked to do this, freeze external work.

## 8. Expense boundary

Fred may absorb trivial local costs during exploration.

Once work requires:
- flights
- hotels
- conferences
- investor dinners
- paid data services
- legal or translation costs
- other meaningful spending

HOPE should approve and cover them.

Do not personally finance HOPE's fundraising.

## 9. Time-allocation boundary

Until equity is documented or the opportunity clearly moves into a higher category, HOPE should consume no more than roughly 10–20% of Fred's productive working time.

## 10. Scope-growth rule

Unpaid contribution may increase only if at least one of these increases with it:
- ownership
- authority
- access
- information advantage
- strategic position
- career value

If responsibility rises while position stays static, stop scope growth.

## 11. Sunk-cost rule

Past effort never justifies future effort.

Every new tranche of work must earn the next tranche.

## 12. Absolute question

If HOPE never raises another dollar and never becomes valuable, was this phase still worth the cash, access, learning, reputation, career value, or relationships Fred received?

If no, do not continue on the same terms.
