# HOPE Fusion — Walk-Away Rules

## Immediate freeze conditions

Freeze new work if:
- website payment becomes problematic
- founders expect major unpaid work immediately
- they ask Fred to make claims he cannot substantiate
- technical evidence is withheld while fundraising is expected
- modeled results are presented as demonstrated
- they want high-value investor introductions before formalizing Fred
- material IP ownership cannot be explained
- equity discussions become evasive after obvious value creation
- the fundraising entity is unclear
- they pressure Fred to finance expenses personally

## Scientific stop conditions

Strong reason to stop if:
- major claims collapse under basic questioning
- neutron claims lack credible diagnostics
- results cannot be reproduced
- internal scientists materially disagree on what was demonstrated
- private evidence is weaker than the public story
- credible independent review is blocked

## Commercial stop conditions

Stop or sharply reduce involvement if:
- Fred's responsibilities grow while authority or ownership does not
- he becomes the international face without formal authority
- 80 unpaid hours are reached without documented equity or comparable commitment
- the six-week review shows no meaningful improvement in Fred's position
- founders repeatedly delay every commitment while accepting Fred's work
- fundraising remains a vague "find us money" exercise
- nobody can explain what the next USD 1M / 10M / 100M buys

## Relationship stop condition

Do not stay merely because the people are pleasant, impressive, or connected.

The relationship must create increasing strategic position.

## Reputation rule

Never spend Fred's credibility faster than HOPE's evidence improves.

## Final rule

The goal is not to prove Fred was right about HOPE.

The goal is to preserve exposure to the upside while exiting quickly if the evidence or relationship stops improving.
