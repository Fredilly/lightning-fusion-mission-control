# HOPE Fusion — Evidence & Knowledge Graph System

## Purpose

Build a structured understanding of HOPE Fusion that connects:

Evidence → Claims → Sources → Scientific Validation → Public Communication → Investor Readiness

The objective is not simply to create a website.

The objective is to create an institutional knowledge system that allows HOPE to communicate accurately with:
- scientists
- research institutions
- strategic partners
- investors
- grant organizations
- media

The website is the first public output of this system.

## Core Principle

Never start with the story. Start with the evidence.

Every important statement should answer:
1. What is the claim?
2. What evidence supports it?
3. Who provided the evidence?
4. When was it produced?
5. Has it been independently verified?
6. What wording is safe to communicate publicly?

## Knowledge Graph Structure

Every important piece of information should become a node connected to related nodes.

Examples:

Patent → Inventor → Owner → Technology claim → Experimental milestone → Publication → Investor relevance

Researcher → Biography → Institution → Publication history → Technical credibility

Experiment → Equipment → Measurement → Result → Claim → Verification level

## Scientific Claim Ledger

Every major scientific statement must be classified.

### DEMONSTRATED
Published or independently verified experimental result.

Record:
- measurement
- methodology
- source
- date

### INTERNALLY OBSERVED
Observed by HOPE but not independently verified.

Safe wording:
"The team has observed..."

### MODELED
Based on simulation, theory, or calculation.

Safe wording:
"Models suggest..."

### PROJECTED
Future expected performance.

Safe wording:
"The team aims to..."

### HYPOTHESIS
Scientific possibility being investigated.

Safe wording:
"The research explores whether..."

## Website Intelligence Map

### Company
Collect:
- official name
- English name
- founding date
- locations
- mission
- legal entity
- contact information

Questions:
- Who is the legal entity?
- Who controls the organization?
- Who approves public statements?

### Scientific Thesis
Collect:
- fusion approach
- scientific principles
- research objectives
- technical differentiation
- current development stage

Questions:
- What is genuinely different?
- What problem is HOPE solving?
- What scientific bottleneck is being attacked?

### Experiments
For every experiment record:
- Name
- Date
- Purpose
- Equipment
- Variables
- Measurements
- Result
- Reproducibility
- Evidence source
- Verification level
- Investor relevance

### Equipment and Laboratory
Collect:
- apparatus photographs
- diagrams
- videos
- experimental environment
- researchers working

Questions:
- Does the physical infrastructure match the claimed research stage?
- What can an external visitor understand immediately?

### Publications
For each publication record:
- Title
- Authors
- Date
- Journal
- DOI
- Research question
- Main finding
- Importance
- Relationship to HOPE
- Verification level

### Patents
For each patent record:
- Title
- Patent number
- Inventors
- Owner
- Filing date
- Jurisdiction
- Current rights
- Technology relevance

Critical question:
- Does the fundraising entity control the IP?

### Research Timeline
For each milestone record:
- Year/date
- Event
- Evidence
- Importance
- Verification level

Include:
- beginning of research
- theoretical developments
- equipment development
- experiments
- publications
- patents
- collaborations
- company formation
- milestones

### Research Team
For each principal researcher or public team member record:
- name
- title
- biography
- academic background
- research areas
- major achievements
- publications
- patents
- institutional relationships
- public photograph

### Collaborations
For each collaboration record:
- institution
- people involved
- type of relationship
- time period
- contribution
- public evidence
- permission to mention publicly

Do not treat an institutional name alone as proof of endorsement.

### Media and External Recognition
For each item record:
- outlet/institution
- date
- link/source
- subject
- claim made
- relevance
- credibility

## 11-Star Design Standard

Do not make a normal research website 10% better.

Benchmark the premium end of:
- research laboratories
- frontier AI labs
- aerospace companies
- advanced-energy companies
- advanced-materials companies
- scientific journals
- elite universities
- architecture studios

Target characteristics:
- very little unnecessary copy
- exceptional typography
- real laboratory imagery
- strong scientific diagrams
- clear information hierarchy
- restrained motion
- useful data visualization
- almost zero hype

Working thought experiment:
If CERN, DeepMind, Apple, and a top scientific journal collaborated on HOPE's website, what would remain?

## Interactive Science & Education

Any interactive scientific model must be grounded in values and relationships approved by Dr. Zheng or the designated scientific reviewer.

For each parameter record:
- name
- symbol
- unit
- representative value
- permitted minimum/maximum
- relationship to other variables
- equation/calculation method
- benchmark/reference value
- physical meaning
- limits/caveats

Never imply that a simplified educational model reproduces the full HOPE experimental system unless that has been explicitly established.

## Diligence Question Log

Questions should emerge naturally from missing connections.

Do not ask everything immediately. Batch high-value questions.

For every question record:
- Question
- Why it matters
- Evidence needed
- Who can answer
- Priority
- Status

Examples generated from website material:
- Patent → Who currently owns it?
- Publication → Has this result been reproduced since publication?
- Plasma image → What experiment produced it?
- Claimed milestone → What measurement establishes it?
- Collaboration → What exactly was the institution's role?
- Current apparatus → What is the next decisive experiment?
- Scientific achievement → Is this a typical result or the best-ever result?

## The Three Skeptical Scientist Test

Ask:

"If three skeptical fusion physicists were here, what experiment or dataset would you show them first?"

Record:
- Concern
- Evidence available
- Missing evidence
- Response

Also ask internally:

"If three skeptical fusion physicists entered the room tomorrow, what would they challenge first?"

## Investor Readiness Layer

Do not build investor materials until the evidence map is sufficiently coherent.

Investors will ask:

### Technology
- What exactly works?
- What has been measured?
- What remains uncertain?
- What is the next decisive experiment?

### Team
- Why this team?
- What unique expertise exists?

### IP
- Who owns the technology?

### Capital
- What has previous funding achieved?
- What does the next dollar unlock?

### Risk
- What can still fail?
- What experiment removes the biggest uncertainty?

## Investor Objection Map

For every objection record:
- Objection
- Who raised it
- Why it matters
- Evidence needed
- Current response
- Updated response

Examples:
- How is this different from existing fusion approaches?
- Why now?
- What has actually been demonstrated?
- What prevents another team from doing this?
- Why does this require private capital?

## Knowledge Graph Rules

### Rule 1
A claim without evidence is a question.

### Rule 2
A source without context is not understanding.

### Rule 3
A result without measurement details is not an investor-grade result.

### Rule 4
A patent without ownership clarity is not an asset.

### Rule 5
A collaboration without defined contribution is not proof.

### Rule 6
A beautiful website cannot compensate for unclear evidence.
